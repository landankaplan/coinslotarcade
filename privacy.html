Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What this site does

Coinslot Arcade is a collection of browser games. The games run entirely in your browser — there are no accounts, no sign-ups, and no data is sent to us to play any game.

High scores

Your high scores are saved using your browser's local storage, on your own device. They are never transmitted to us or to anyone else, and they disappear if you clear your browser's site data.

Advertising and cookies

This site may display ads served by third-party advertising partners. Those partners may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to serve ads and measure their performance. We don't control how our advertising partners use that data — their own privacy policies govern it.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to opt out of personalized advertising. For Google-served ads, you can manage ad personalization at adssettings.google.com. You can also block cookies generally in your browser settings.

Children's privacy

This site does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If we display ads, ad personalization may be limited or disabled for visitors believed to be children, in line with our advertising partners' policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site changes, including if we add or change advertising partners. Check back here for the current version.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the site owner.
